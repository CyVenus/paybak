# Paybak privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Paybak is an open-source expense-splitting app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Your ledger stays on your device

Everything you enter stays on your phone: your name, friends, groups, projects, expenses, payments and receipt photos. Paybak has no server and no account system, and it does not upload this data anywhere. Deleting the app, or using **Delete account** in the app, removes it.

## Purchases (RevenueCat)

Paybak Pro subscriptions are handled by [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com) and by Apple's App Store or Google Play. To manage your subscription, RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID
- your purchase and subscription status
- basic device information, such as platform, app version and country

RevenueCat does not receive your ledger, contacts or photos. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** used only to attach receipts and to read them on the device.
- **Contacts:** used only when you choose to add friends from your contacts. They are read on the device and never uploaded.
- **Notifications:** used only for the reminders you schedule.

## Children

Paybak is not directed at children under 13.
