# Paybak terms of use

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

1. **The app.** Paybak helps you keep track of shared expenses. It is a record-keeping tool. It does not move money, and it is not a bank or a payment service.
2. **Your records.** You are responsible for what you enter. Balances are calculated from your records and the bundled exchange rates, and may not match what you actually owe.
3. **Paybak Pro.** Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) purchased through the App Store or Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. It renews automatically until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in your store account settings, or from **Profile › Paybak Pro › Manage subscription**. The store's refund rules apply.
